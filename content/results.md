LSC'26 took place on June 16, 2026 at ICMR'26. 

32 users competed live across 21 tasks spanning three categories: Known-Item Search (KIS), Ad-hoc Video Search (AVS), and Question & Answer (QA), which can be see in the [Tasks](../tasks/) page.

Congratulations to all participants!
