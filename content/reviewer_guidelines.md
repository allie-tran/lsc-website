Thank you for serving as a reviewer for the Lifelog Search Challenge (LSC). Your expertise ensures the quality of the workshop and helps authors improve their systems and research. Please follow these guidelines when evaluating submissions.

## General Principles
* **Be Constructive:** Provide feedback that helps authors improve their work. Even for papers you recommend rejecting, provide specific suggestions for improvement.
* **Expert Judgment:** Do NOT use generative AI tools (e.g., ChatGPT) to generate the content of your review. The evaluation must reflect your own independent expert judgment.

---

## Review Structure
Please organize your review into the following sections:
1.  **Paper Summary:** A brief description of the submission and its main contributions.
2.  **Strengths:** Clear points that support the acceptance of the paper.
3.  **Weaknesses:** Areas where the paper lacks clarity, technical soundness, or empirical support.
4.  **Justification:** A summary of how you weighed the strengths against the weaknesses to reach your final recommendation.

---

## Track-Specific Guidelines

Please double-check that the track you are reviewing. On OpenReview, this is the first field on the paper's page. The evaluation criteria differ based on the track:

### 1. Search System Track
*Papers in this track describe the interactive systems that will compete in the live LSC competition.*

* **System Architecture:** Does the paper clearly explain the indexing, retrieval, and UI components? Does it describe how it handles the LSC multimodal dataset?
* **Interactive Features:** Since LSC is a "human-in-the-loop" challenge, is the user interface well-described? How does it facilitate efficient browsing or search refinement?
* **Reproducibility:** Is there enough technical detail for another researcher to understand the system's logic?
* **Innovation:** Does the system introduce new techniques?

### 2. General Research Track
*Papers in this track present broader research contributions in lifelogging that may not involve the live competition. We focus on the diversity of research topics and methodologies, as well as discussion of implications for the lifelogging community.*

* **Relevance**: Does the paper address a problem that is important or interesting to the lifelogging community (e.g., data management, user experience, privacy)?
* **Discussion**: Does the paper provide a thoughtful discussion of the implications of the research for the broader lifelogging ecosystem?
* **Methodology:** Is the research design sound? Are the datasets used (LSC or otherwise) appropriate for the claims made?
* **Clarity:** Is the paper well-written and organized? Are the contributions clearly stated?

---

## Conflict of Interest & Anonymity
* **Single-Blind Review:** LSC typically uses a single-blind process. Reviewers are known to the chairs, but authors do not know their reviewers. 
* **Conflicts:** If you have a conflict of interest with a paper (e.g., being at the same institution or a recent collaborator), please notify the organisers immediately.
