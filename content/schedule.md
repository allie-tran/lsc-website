### Session Details

* **Long Presentations:** Each of the 4 long-form presentations will consist of a 12-minute talk followed by 3 minutes for Q&A.
* **Short Presentations:** These lightning talks (5 minutes each) are designed to provide a rapid overview of the participating search systems and novel lifelogging research. Note: This could be a video if the team wishes to do so.
* **System Testing:** A 30-minute window for teams to verify their connection to the evaluation server and ensure their interactive search engines are fully operational.
* **Live Contest:** The competition will involve solving approximately 24 interactive search topics.
